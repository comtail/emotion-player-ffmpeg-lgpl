2. Locally processed information

EMOTION Player plays media from sources you add, such as M3U playlists and Xtream-compatible services. Data processed on your device may include source URLs, login credentials, media catalogues, programme guides, artwork, favourites, playback progress and preferences.

According to the current technical project description, credentials are encrypted locally. The exact implementation, exclusions from device backups and deletion controls must be verified against the final distributed build.

3. Connections to third-party sources

The app connects to servers you specify or which are referenced by your sources to retrieve playlists, media, streams, artwork and programme guides. These servers may receive your IP address, requested URLs and any credentials needed for authentication. Their operators are independently responsible for their own data processing. Unencrypted HTTP connections may expose information, including credentials, in transit.

Sample content (optional): If you tap “View sample content”, the app downloads a sample playlist of freely licensed films and music from GitHub Pages (comtail.github.io) and adds it as a source. Preview images also come from comtail.github.io; video and audio files come from github.com, redirected to release-assets.githubusercontent.com or objects.githubusercontent.com. All of these servers are operated by GitHub, Inc. (privacy statement: docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies) and receive your IP address, the app identifier and the requested address. Nothing is loaded without that tap. You can remove the sample source like any other source.

4. External players

When you select another installed player, the app may pass the media URL and title to that app. Xtream stream URLs can contain credentials. The external player may process or retain these details. A warning before sensitive transfers is part of the final release verification.

5. Accounts, analytics and purchases

According to the current project description, Corevant Software does not operate a media server for playback and the app has no proprietary user accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs. These statements depend on verification of the final distributed build.

When Google Play distributes or processes a purchase of the app, Google handles relevant data under its own privacy policy.

6. Open-source information

FFmpeg source and licensing materials are linked at github.com/comtail/emotion-player-ffmpeg-lgpl. Visiting GitHub is subject to GitHub's privacy practices.

7. Legal grounds

Where Corevant Software is the controller, processing necessary to provide app functions or perform a contract may be based on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; legal obligations may be based on Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR. Necessary access to and storage of information on a device may fall under Section 25(2)(2) TDDDG. Any additional processing may require another legal basis.

8. Retention and deletion

Depending on supported app controls, sources and local settings can be changed or deleted. Uninstalling the app removes ordinary local app data; device backups may be subject to separate operating-system and app configurations, which must be checked before release.

9. Your rights

Subject to legal conditions, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability or object to processing under Articles 15–21 GDPR. Contact info@corevant.de. You may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority; the Bavarian Data Protection Authority (BayLDA) generally supervises private entities established in Bavaria.

This policy will be updated if the app's functionality, services or data flows change.
